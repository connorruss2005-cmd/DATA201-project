Deliverable 5 - Data Analysis

Overview:
Executed exploratory data analysis (EDA) and generated four presentation-ready visualisations using Python 
(`pandas` and `matplotlib`) based on the joined AirBnB and 
Christchurch rental bond dataset 

Key Tasks Completed & Findings:

Question 1: Christchurch Central Median AirBnB Price
- Filtered dataset for SA2 location code `326600` (Christchurch Central).
- Calculated and verified the median nightly AirBnB price ($238.00/night).

Question 2: Short-Term vs. Long-Term Nightly Price Gap Analysis
- Converted weekly rental bond rates (`median_rent`) into equivalent daily rates (`median_rent / 7`).
- Computed the daily price premium per property (`AirBnB price - Bond daily rate`).
- Generated two price gap bar charts sorted from largest to smallest difference:
    - `bar_price_gap_all_areas.png`: Full distribution across all 50+ Christchurch SA2 area codes.
    - `bar_price_gap_top15.png`: Zoomed-in view of the Top 15 highest-volume areas for slide readability.

Question 3: Housing Market Volume Comparison (AirBnB vs. Active Bonds)
- Aggregated unique AirBnB listing counts against active tenancy bond records per SA2 area code.
- Generated two side-by-side grouped bar charts comparing short-term visitor supply against long-term residential housing supply:
    -`property_counts_all_areas.png`: Full dataset comparison across all 50+ area codes.
    -`property_counts_top15.png`: Focused comparison of the Top 15 areas highlighting tourist hubs (e.g., Christchurch Central) versus long-term residential/student suburbs (e.g., Upper Riccarton, Addington).
